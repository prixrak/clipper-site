Clipper

Clipper turns long Twitch and Kick broadcasts into short vertical highlights and posts them to the operator's own YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and X accounts.

What it does

  • Downloads a public VOD the operator chooses, transcribes it and ranks the moments a viewer would want to see again.
  • Renders each moment as a 9:16 clip with captions, a credit to the streamer and a link back to the full stream.
  • Publishes the clip to accounts the operator has connected, on a schedule the operator sets, and reads back view counts to report earnings from creator programmes such as Whop Content Rewards.

Who uses it

Clipper is a self-hosted tool run by its operator for the operator's own accounts. It does not post on behalf of third parties, does not sell access, and does not collect data from viewers of the published clips.

Connected platforms

Each platform is connected through its official API with OAuth. Clipper requests only the permissions needed to upload a video and read its public statistics. Tokens stay on the operator's machine. See the privacy policy and terms of service.
